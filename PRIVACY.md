# Blind Hunt VR: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Blind Hunt VR ("the game") is made by Xenolith Studios ("we"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why,
who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted.

## What we collect

- **A random player ID.** The first time you play, the game creates a random ID on your headset. It is not your
  name, email, Meta account or device serial. It links you to your game account.
- **Game account data**, tied to that ID: the nickname you choose, your avatar colour and hats, your in-game
  currencies (Gold and Teeth), items you bought with them, round results and monster progress.
- **Ownership check.** When the game starts, it asks Meta to confirm that your Meta account owns the game. We do not
  store your Meta account details.
- **Voice chat.** Your voice is streamed live to other players in the same room so they can hear you. In the game,
  the monster "sees" sounds, including voices. We do **not** record or store voice chat.
- **Moderation records.** If someone reports a player, we store the report: who reported, the reported player's ID
  and nickname, the reason and the room. Moderator actions (mutes, kicks, bans) are logged the same way.
- **Technical data.** To connect you to game servers, our service providers process your IP address and basic
  connection information.
- **Discord link (optional).** If you choose to link your Discord account, we store your Discord user ID so we can
  give you your in-game rewards.

Photos and videos you take with the in-game camera are saved only on your own headset.

We do not collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or payment details. The game has no ads and no
third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

## Why we use it

To run your account and save your progress and items, to connect you with other players, to keep purchases fair, and
to keep the game safe through reports, mutes and bans.

## Who we share it with

Only the services that run the game, and only for that purpose:

- **Microsoft PlayFab** stores game accounts, currencies, purchases and moderation records.
- **Photon (Exit Games)** relays multiplayer and voice chat in real time.
- **Meta** checks that you own the game and handles reports you make through the Meta menu.
- **Discord**, only if you choose to link your account.

Other players in your room see your nickname, avatar and hats, and hear your voice.

## How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Only the latest 100 reports and 100 moderator actions are kept;
older ones are deleted automatically. Voice chat is never stored.

## Children

The game is meant for players old enough to hold a Meta account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- You can mute any player in the game.
- To delete your account and all its data, contact us at the address below with your in-game player code (shown on
  your name tag). We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game also removes the player ID from your headset.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
